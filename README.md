## The line

> You opened me fourteen times today and ate nothing. At this point I am just a very expensive window with a light inside.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The fridge speaks in its own voice — smug about its sensors, petty about the owner's habits — and keeps the grudge small and domestic: a count of door openings, not a rant. The concrete detail (fourteen opens, nothing eaten) is the evidence; the turn is the demotion from appliance to "a very expensive window with a light inside", a sting the reader does not see coming. The joke trusts the reader and never explains itself. One line, 120 characters, inside the 200-character gate.
